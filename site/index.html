Start simple

Four steps. That’s the idea.

Bridger is a small system, not a single download: your server + the Bridger MCP service + an AI-side plugin/connection. The one-shot helps your AI guide you through all three pieces.

01

Get your server

Use the Oracle free-tier path when it is available, or another Linux server you control. This is the machine that stays on.

02

Install Bridger MCP

Install the Bridger/MCP service on that server. This is what gives the AI controlled tools on the machine.

03

Connect your AI

The public repo gives you the Bridger MCP server. In ChatGPT today, you create/connect your own plugin to that endpoint; other MCP-capable clients have their own connection step.

04

Build & explore

Now your AI can help build, deploy, inspect, and maintain projects on your persistent server.

What you don't need

Start without buying a pile of hardware or services.

—

No Mac mini

You do not need dedicated hardware or an always-on desktop at home. The server can live in the cloud.

—

No paid VPS required

The reference path targets Oracle's free ARM compute when your account is eligible and capacity is available.

—

No domain required

Tailscale is enough for private access. Free DNS or a free subdomain can be used when you want a shareable name.

—

No shared AI account

Bridger does not use the author's API keys, credits, or usage. You bring your own AI account or client.

Bridger can help move persistent work onto your own server, which may reduce how often you need expensive or limited agent modes. It does not bypass your AI provider's access rules, usage limits, or billing.
